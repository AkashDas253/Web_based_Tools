HOW TO PLAY

Read the clues.

Numbers show how many mines touch that square, including diagonals. Use them to work out where the mines are hiding.

  • Reveal: Select a covered square to uncover it.
  • Flag: Mark a square you think contains a mine.
  • Empty squares: Reveal a surrounding area automatically.
  • Win: Reveal every safe square without hitting a mine.

Your first revealed square is always safe. On touch screens, use the Reveal and Flag controls.
